About

Engineer by training, designer by habit.

I work across the whole stack — Java, Groovy/Grails, PHP and JavaScript on the back end, React, Next.js, TypeScript and Tailwind CSS on the front end, and Flutter for mobile. Most of my professional work happens inside large CMS-driven platforms (Grails, Adobe AEM, Sitecore) where reliability, clean data and careful change management matter.

What sets me apart is that I design the interfaces I build. I've led UI for CMS editors and product information tools, translated Figma designs into production pages, and I still take on branding and logo work. For me, great software isn't only about performance — it's about how it feels to use.

I hold a master's degree in Computer Science with a focus on machine learning, and I'm a certified professional engineer (Ir., IPP).
